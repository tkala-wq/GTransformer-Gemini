# 04 · Additives and Ingredients of Concern

**Topic:** Flagged ingredients and additives, their regulatory status and limits, and their risk tier.
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-14

> **Regulatory status changes.** Before publishing a rating, confirm the current status at the source named in each row.
> † = the rating depends on a **proposed** source (EU Commission or SCCS, CDSCO, AYUSH, FTC, OEHHA) listed in `02-approved-sources.md` §5. Until that source is approved, use the fallback tier shown in brackets.
> Tiers follow `01-scoring-methodology.md` §2. **This is not medical advice.**

## 1. Personal care (soaps, shampoos, oils, lotions)

### 1.1 High

| Ingredient (label names) | Concern | Regulatory status and limits | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mercury compounds** (calomel, mercurous chloride, "mercurio") | Kidney and nerve damage | Prohibited in US cosmetics except trace amounts (<1 ppm) and some eye-area preservatives (≤65 ppm) | FDA, 21 CFR 700.13 |
| **Hydroquinone** in OTC skin lighteners | Skin damage (ochronosis) | No longer legally marketable as an OTC drug in the US since the CARES Act (2020) | FDA |
| **Formaldehyde** (formalin, methylene glycol) | Known human carcinogen (by inhalation); sensitiser | IARC Group 1. FDA has listed a proposed ban on formaldehyde in hair-smoothing products on its regulatory agenda (*check status*) | IARC; FDA |
| **Benzene** (contaminant) | Known human carcinogen | IARC Group 1. FDA recalls of aerosol dry shampoos and sprays (2021–22). Limit 2 ppm where unavoidable (ICH Q3C as adopted by FDA) | IARC; FDA recalls |
| **Methylene chloride, chloroform, vinyl chloride, bithionol, halogenated salicylanilides, zirconium in aerosols** | Various toxicities | Prohibited in US cosmetics | FDA, 21 CFR 700.11–700.19 |
| **Hexachlorophene** | Neurotoxic | Restricted; prohibited in products for mucous membranes | FDA, 21 CFR 250.250 |
| **Lead above 10 ppm** | Neurotoxic, especially for children | FDA guidance (2022) recommends ≤10 ppm lead in lip products and externally applied cosmetics | FDA |
| **Asbestos in talc** (contaminant) | Known human carcinogen | IARC Group 1. FDA proposed a talc-testing rule under MoCRA (Dec 2024; *check status*) | IARC; FDA |
| **Coal tar** (as a cosmetic ingredient) | Coal-tar pitch is IARC Group 1 | Allowed in the US only as an **OTC anti-dandruff/psoriasis drug at 0.5–5%** (21 CFR 358.710). Banned in EU cosmetics† | IARC; FDA |

### 1.2 Moderate

| Ingredient | Concern | Regulatory status and limits | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pyrithione zinc (zinc pyrithione, ZPT)** | Classified as toxic to reproduction (CMR 1B) in the EU | **US:** FDA OTC anti-dandruff active, 1–2% rinse-off, 0.1–0.25% leave-on (21 CFR 358.710). **EU:** banned in cosmetics since 1 Mar 2022 (Reg. 2021/1902)† | FDA; EU Commission† *(fallback: Low)* |
| **Formaldehyde releasers**: DMDM hydantoin, quaternium-15, imidazolidinyl urea, diazolidinyl urea, bronopol (2-bromo-2-nitropropane-1,3-diol), sodium hydroxymethylglycinate | Release formaldehyde; sensitisers | EU requires the warning "releases formaldehyde" above 0.001% (Reg. 2022/1181)† | IARC (formaldehyde); EU† *(fallback: Moderate via IARC)* |
| **Methylisothiazolinone (MI) / methylchloroisothiazolinone (MCI)** | Strong skin sensitisers | EU: MI banned in leave-on; rinse-off ≤0.0015% (Reg. 2017/1224); MCI/MI mixture rinse-off only† | EU† *(fallback: No authoritative data found)* |
| **Fragrance / parfum / perfume / aroma** | Undisclosed mixture; common cause of cosmetic allergy; may contain phthalates | MoCRA (2022) requires FDA to set fragrance-allergen labeling (*rule pending*) | FDA (allergens in cosmetics; MoCRA) |
| **Diethyl phthalate (DEP)** | Endocrine-disruption research | FDA: no compelling evidence of risk as used in cosmetics; under NIEHS research | FDA (phthalates page); NIEHS |
| **Dibutyl phthalate (DBP), DEHP** | Reproductive toxicity | Banned in EU cosmetics†; DEHP IARC Group 2B | IARC; EU† *(fallback: Moderate via IARC for DEHP)* |
| **1,4-Dioxane** (contaminant of SLES, "-eth" and PEG ingredients) | Possible carcinogen | IARC 2B; EPA "likely carcinogenic to humans". New York State caps it at 1 ppm (context only) | IARC; EPA |
| **Cocamide DEA** | Possible carcinogen | IARC 2B (2013); Prop 65 listed† | IARC |
| **Triclosan, triclocarban** | Antimicrobial resistance; hormone effects | FDA final rule (2016) removed them from OTC consumer antiseptic washes | FDA |
| **Butylparaben, propylparaben** | Weak oestrogen activity | EU: combined ≤0.14%; isopropyl-, isobutyl-, phenyl-, benzyl- and pentylparaben banned†. FDA: no evidence of harm at current levels | FDA; EU† *(fallback: Low)* |
| **PFAS** ("perfluoro-", "polyfluoro-", PTFE) | Persistent "forever chemicals" | FDA PFAS-in-cosmetics report under MoCRA; EPA PFAS actions | EPA; FDA |
| **Untreated or mildly treated mineral oil** | Carcinogen | IARC Group 1 (highly refined cosmetic grade is Group 3 → Low) | IARC |

### 1.3 Low

| Ingredient | Concern | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Sodium lauryl sulfate (SLS)** | Skin and eye irritant at high concentration or long contact | FDA (cosmetic ingredients); PubChem |
| **Sodium laureth sulfate (SLES)** | Mild irritant; 1,4-dioxane watch-point (score only if a test or recall names the product) | EPA (dioxane); PubChem |
| **Sodium C14-16 olefin sulfonate** | Skin and eye irritant (a sulfonate, not a sulfate, so "sulfate-free" can still be true) | PubChem (GHS hazard classifications) |
| **Cocamidopropyl betaine** | Contact allergy, mostly from impurities | FDA (allergens in cosmetics) |
| **Phenoxyethanol** | Irritation; EU max 1%† | EU† *(fallback: No authoritative data found)* |
| **Methyl- and ethylparaben** | Low concern at permitted levels | FDA |
| **Synthetic colours** (FD&C Blue 1, CI 42090, etc.) | Must be FDA-listed colour additives; rare allergy | FDA colour additive regulations (21 CFR Parts 73/74/82) |
| **Highly refined mineral oil / paraffinum liquidum** | Not classifiable (IARC Group 3) | IARC |
| **Hydrolysed wheat protein** | Allergen caution for people with wheat allergy (not a toxicity rating) | FDA (allergens in cosmetics) |
| **Sesame oil** (topical) | Allergen caution; sesame is a US major food allergen (FASTER Act 2021) | FDA |

### 1.4 Environmental flags (reported in Claim Integrity and the verdict)

| Item | Status | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Plastic microbeads (polyethylene, polypropylene) in rinse-off products | Banned in the US (Microbead-Free Waters Act 2015) | FDA |
| BPA in packaging | See §2.2 | FDA; EFSA |
| Ethoxylated surfactants | Dioxane watch-point; aquatic toxicity | EPA Safer Choice |

## 2. Supplements and food

### 2.1 High

| Ingredient | Concern | Status | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DMAA** (1,3-dimethylamylamine, geranium extract) | Heart attack, stroke | Not a lawful dietary ingredient | FDA |
| **Hidden drugs** (sibutramine, sildenafil, steroids, SARMs) | Serious adverse events | FDA tainted-products database; warning letters | FDA |
| **Pure or highly concentrated powdered caffeine** | Overdose risk | FDA guidance (2018): unlawful in bulk retail packages | FDA |
| **Lead, mercury or arsenic in Ayurvedic or herbal products** (rasa shastra preparations, "bhasma") | Heavy-metal poisoning | CDC MMWR reports of lead poisoning linked to Ayurvedic medicines; FDA warnings | CDC; FDA |
| **Brominated vegetable oil (BVO)** | Thyroid and other effects | FDA revoked its authorisation (2024) | FDA |
| **FD&C Red No. 3 (erythrosine)** | Thyroid tumours in animals | FDA revoked authorisation in food (Jan 2025; compliance Jan 2027) and ingested drugs (2028) | FDA |
| **Any nutrient above the NIH ODS Upper Limit** at the suggested daily dose | Toxicity (e.g. preformed vitamin A, iron, vitamin B6, selenium) | Adult ULs: vitamin A 3,000 mcg RAE; vitamin D 100 mcg (4,000 IU); iron 45 mg; zinc 40 mg; B6 100 mg; selenium 400 mcg; niacin (supplements) 35 mg | NIH ODS |

### 2.2 Moderate

| Ingredient | Concern | Status | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Titanium dioxide (E171)** | Genotoxicity could not be ruled out | EFSA 2021: no longer considered safe; banned as a food additive in the EU (2022). FDA permits ≤1% by weight | EFSA; FDA |
| **Bisphenol A (BPA)** in containers | Endocrine and immune effects | EFSA 2023: TDI lowered to 0.2 ng/kg bw/day; EU food-contact ban (Reg. 2024/3190). FDA: safe at current levels in food packaging; banned in baby bottles and infant-formula packaging | EFSA; FDA |
| **Potassium bromate** | Possible carcinogen | IARC 2B; banned in India (FSSAI, 2016); permitted in the US | IARC; FSSAI |
| **Green tea extract (high-dose EGCG)** | Liver injury | Documented in LiverTox | NIH LiverTox |
| **Ashwagandha** | Rare liver injury | Documented in LiverTox | NIH LiverTox |
| **Turmeric/curcumin with piperine** | Rare liver injury | Documented in LiverTox | NIH LiverTox |
| **Inorganic arsenic in rice-based ingredients** (rice protein, rice syrup) | Contaminant | FDA action level for infant rice cereal: 100 ppb. Score only with product data; otherwise Low watch-point | FDA |
| **Aspartame** | Possible carcinogen (hazard) | IARC 2B (2023); JECFA ADI unchanged at 40 mg/kg bw. Phenylketonuria warning required | IARC; JECFA; FDA |

### 2.3 Low

| Ingredient | Concern | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Sucralose, acesulfame-K, stevia glycosides** | WHO (2023) conditionally recommends against non-sugar sweeteners for weight control | WHO; FDA (approved/GRAS) |
| **Caffeine** (in normal doses) | FDA: up to 400 mg/day is generally not linked to negative effects in healthy adults; extra care in pregnancy | FDA |
| **Carrageenan** | Digestive-tolerance questions | JECFA; FDA (permitted) |
| **Artificial flavours, "natural and artificial flavours"** | Undisclosed composition | FDA (21 CFR 101.22) |
| **Maltodextrin** | High glycaemic filler | FDA (GRAS) |
| **Sugar alcohols** (sorbitol, mannitol, erythritol) | Laxative effect | FDA ("excess consumption may have a laxative effect" statement for sorbitol/mannitol) |

## 3. Major allergens (always flag, never score as toxicity)

| United States (FALCPA + FASTER Act) | India (FSSAI Labelling & Display Regulations 2020) |
|---|---|
| Milk | Milk |
| Eggs | Eggs |
| Fish | Fish |
| Crustacean shellfish | Crustacean |
| Tree nuts | Tree nuts |
| Peanuts | Peanuts |
| Wheat | Cereals containing gluten (wheat, rye, barley, oats, spelt) |
| Soybeans | Soybeans |
| Sesame (from 1 Jan 2023) | Sulphite (≥10 mg/kg) |

In cosmetics, look for protein derivatives: *hydrolyzed wheat protein*, *milk protein / lactis proteinum*, *soy protein*, *sesamum indicum oil*, *arachis hypogaea (peanut) oil*, *nut oils*.

## 4. Other names for sugar and salt

**Sugar:** sucrose, glucose, dextrose, fructose, high-fructose corn syrup, corn syrup solids, maltose, malt syrup, rice syrup, brown rice syrup, cane juice, evaporated cane juice, invert sugar, honey, agave, molasses, fruit-juice concentrate, coconut sugar, jaggery (gur), khandsari.

**Sodium:** sodium chloride, monosodium glutamate (MSG), sodium bicarbonate, sodium citrate, disodium phosphate, sodium benzoate, sodium caseinate, rock salt (sendha namak), black salt (kala namak).

## 5. Who should be careful (only when an approved source supports it)

| Group | Watch for | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **Pregnancy** | Preformed vitamin A above the UL; caffeine; herbal supplements without safety data | NIH ODS; FDA |
| **Children** | Lead or mercury in imported cosmetics and Ayurvedic remedies; adult-dose supplements; caffeine | CDC; FDA |
| **Allergy sufferers** | Fragrance, MI/MCI, cocamidopropyl betaine, protein derivatives, the major allergens | FDA |
| **Phenylketonuria** | Aspartame | FDA |
| **Liver conditions** | Green tea extract, ashwagandha, turmeric/curcumin supplements | NIH LiverTox |
